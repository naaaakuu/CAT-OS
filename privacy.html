CAT OS Privacy Policy

Nakul Creations · Effective 9 October 2026

CAT OS is a study app for the verbal section of India's Common Admission Test. This policy covers the CAT OS Android app (com.nakulcreations.catos) and the web version at naaaakuu.github.io/CAT-OS.

The short version

  • Your progress, answers and settings stay on your phone. We have no servers and no accounts, and we never receive them.
  • The Android app shows an ad only when you tap "Watch a short video" to open a full explanation. Those ads come from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to serve them.
  • CAT OS Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in CAT OS (the passages you read, your answers and timings, the words you keep, your village and its friends, your settings) is stored in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose and nowhere else.

Android may include app data in your device backup to your Google account if you have Android Backup turned on. That is controlled by you in Android settings and handled by Google, not by us.

Ads (Android app only)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for rewarded video ads. An ad loads and plays only after you choose to watch one. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • how you interact with the ad, and app diagnostics such as crash and performance data.

How Google uses this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Google, Ads.

The web version shows no ads.

Purchases

CAT OS Pro (a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase) is processed entirely by Google Play under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. The app only asks Google Play, on your device, whether your Google account owns Pro, and remembers the answer on your device so Pro works offline.

Internet connection

The free Android app checks, on your device, whether you are connected to the internet, because ads need a connection. Nothing about your connection is sent to us.

What we share

We do not collect personal data, so we do not sell or share it. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob (ads, as above) and Google Play (downloads and purchases). The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits under its own privacy statement.

Children

CAT OS is made for adults preparing for the CAT and other management entrance exams. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Settings, Start over deletes everything CAT OS has stored on your device. Uninstalling the app does the same. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete; for data collected by Google for ads, use your Google account and Android ad settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
