CAT OS Privacy Policy

Nakul Creations · Updated 9 October 2026

CAT OS is an advanced English game: reading, reasoning and vocabulary in a little village that grows as you learn. This policy covers the CAT OS Android app (com.nakulcreations.catos) and the web version at naaaakuu.github.io/CAT-OS, wherever you use them.

The short version

  • Your progress, answers and settings live on your device. Unless you sign in, we never receive them.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, a copy of your progress is kept in your account so you can bring it to another phone. You can delete it, and the account, in the app at any time.
  • The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used, and Google AdMob for the one kind of ad: a video you choose to watch to open a full explanation.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before ads or analytics use your data, and you can change your answer in Settings, Privacy choices.
  • CAT OS Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in CAT OS (the passages you read, your answers and timings, the words you keep, your village and its friends, your settings) is stored in the app's own storage on your device. If you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose and nowhere else.

Android may include app data in your device backup to your Google account if you have Android Backup turned on. That is controlled by you in Android settings and handled by Google, not by us.

Signing in with Google (Android app, optional)

If you tap Sign in with Google in Settings, Google tells the app your account's name and email address, and Google Firebase Authentication gives you a CAT OS account with its own user ID. From then on, whenever you leave the app, a copy of your progress (the same data as an exported backup: your village and its name, your answers, finished runs, the words you keep and your settings) is saved to Google Cloud Firestore under that account. Only your signed-in account can read or write it.

We use this only to give your progress back to you, for example on a new phone. It is not used for ads, not sold and not shared. It is kept until you delete it (see below). It travels encrypted and is stored by Google in the United States.

Analytics (Android app)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how CAT OS is used so we can make it better: which screens are opened, installs and app updates, sign-ins, shares, purchases and ad views. To do this Google collects an app instance identifier, device information (model, Android version, language), an approximate location derived from your IP address, and crash and performance data. Analytics data is not linked to your name or email. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it follows the choice you make in the consent form. The web version has no analytics.

Ads (Android app only)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for rewarded video ads. An ad loads and plays only after you choose to watch one. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • how you interact with the ad, and app diagnostics such as crash and performance data.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) asks first, and you can accept, refuse, or choose purpose by purpose; Settings, Privacy choices brings it back whenever you want to change your answer. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Google, Ads. How Google uses this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The web version shows no ads.

Purchases

CAT OS Pro (a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase) is processed entirely by Google Play under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. The app only asks Google Play, on your device, whether your Google account owns Pro, and remembers the answer on your device so Pro works offline.

Internet connection

The free Android app checks, on your device, whether you are connected to the internet, because ads need a connection. Nothing about your connection is sent to us.

What we share

We do not sell personal data. The only third parties involved are Google (Firebase for sign-in, the cloud save and analytics; AdMob for ads; Google Play for downloads and purchases), each acting under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits under its own privacy statement.

Your choices and rights

You can use CAT OS without signing in. You can export your progress as a file, sign out, delete your account, change your consent choice, and opt out of personalised ads at any time. Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to the address below; if you are in the EEA or the UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings, Google account, Delete account. Google asks you to pick your account once more to confirm, and then your cloud save and your CAT OS account are deleted at once. The progress on your phone stays until you use Settings, Start over, or uninstall the app.

Without the app: email voranakul1@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my CAT OS account". We delete the account and its cloud save within 30 days and reply to confirm. Nothing is kept afterwards, except that analytics data, which is not linked to your account, expires on Google Analytics' own retention schedule.

Without an account there is nothing on our side to delete: Settings, Start over deletes everything CAT OS has stored on your device, and uninstalling does the same. For data Google collects for ads, use your Google account and Android ad settings.

Children

CAT OS is made for adults and older students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
