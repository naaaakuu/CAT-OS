# Architecture (current)

Entry: `index.html` → `src/app.js` (router, boot, `APP_VERSION`, scene audio). Hash routes; modules register their own routes. Cold open budget: `tools/module-graph.mjs`.

```
records (IndexedDB, via StorageAdapter)
   → src/world/state.js   learning state derived from records
   → src/pets/economy.js  derivePets(): Glow, levels, stages, mood, today's three, fire
   → src/home/*           village screen;  src/world/screens/*  rooms, rounds, results
```

## Village (`src/home/`, `src/pets/`)
- One painting (`assets/art/home-world-v1.png`, 1536x1024), full detail, never zooms; phones scroll sideways. The campfire (down to y 950) must never sit under the bottom button: `lowestCam()` in `village.js` lifts the camera, and `.cw-dock` (home.css) is an overlay with the button low-centre (portrait) or lower-right (landscape).
- `home/village.js` camera, HUD, big Help button (Chai's reads "Visit Chai"), today's three, Chai's door (he has no card: a tap on his house, on him, or on the big button runs `enterLibrary`, which shuts `.cw-door` over the village and routes to `LIBRARY_HREF` in `pets/next.js`; `startHref` is where every "Help <friend>" link goes), party, intro, the grown painting (bake on load and when houses grow), the house-grew moment. `home/life.js` gaits, chores, greetings, voices, the scene canvas (viewport-sized, moved with the camera, culled; glows are stamped sprites) and the paced loop (60 max, 30 on a struggling device). `home/cards.js` friend/fire/level/every-subject/settings cards and village-level decorations. `home/motion.js` + generated `motion-atlas.js` = the living painting (44 baked patches; `house`/`from` keep a machine still until its house wakes; off-screen patches pause).
- Houses grow with their own section, which is their friend's (`economy.js houseStages`, 0-10; each spot is its friend's id). `home/houses.js`: `HOUSE_ART` (per-house mask, windows, lamps, chimney, flowers, keepsake, garland, perch, wonders, crown), `bakeVillage` (grading + dark windows + still props into a canvas laid over `.cw-art`), `gradeAtlas` (the motion atlas graded to match), `createHouseLife` (everything that moves, drawn on the scene canvas: plain paint, then one hour-tint pass, then additive lights). `home/paint.js` is the brush (cached 3x sprites, glow/glyph sprites). Copy per stage: `pets.js HOUSE_GROWTH`. Gate hook: `.cw.__village.houses(stages)` shows any stages (looks only).
- `pets/pets.js` roster, HOUSES (subject signs), ages, lines (`LINES`: hello, missed, thanks, cheer, grow by seed; `muse`, `arrive`, `arriveNight` dealt as a no-repeat deck by `dealLine`, memory passed to `life.js` as `memory`), stories, `SIGNATURE` voices, `MODULE_PET`/`PLACE_PET`. `pets/visits.js` (`VISITS`) is what happens when one friend calls on another: 18 scenes of lines and chores that `life.js` plays (`startVisit`, `runVisit`; one social call at a time with `chats`; `root.dataset.visit` names the scene running; `__village.callOn(id)` starts one for the gates). `pets/paths.js` coordinates in painting space: the walk graph (`NODES`, `EDGES`, `route`), `HOMES` (a friend's node, and `side`, where a visitor stands beside it), `CHATS` (neighbouring plaza pairs). Friends stand only on nodes and walk only along edges (`life.js walkTo`; DEV-NOTES §Village). `pets/sprite.js` + `sheets.js` baked sprite sheets (adult and baby), hats, rig. `pets/next.js` each friend's next activity.
- Art is baked by `tools/bake-pets.mjs` (`--baby` for baby sheets) and `tools/bake-motion.mjs`. Change PATCHES → rerun bake-motion + build-precache.
- New subject for a friend: `MODULE_PET` and `PLACE_PET` in `pets.js`, a Glow/progress source in `economy.js`, an entry in `next.js`.

## Economy (`src/pets/`)
- `glow.js` holds every number (`GLOW`). `economy.js` derives; `progress.js` has `levelFor` (subject ladders) and `achievementsFor` (10 whole-app achievements). Nothing here is stored.
- Friends: Toffee (Gauntlet + fire), Chai owl (Reading), Matcha sprout (Vocabulary), Mochi pebble (PS), Ginger fox (PJ), Mallow cloud (OOO), Sesame mouse (PC, the clock tower), Biscuit calico cat (SP, the rose cottage). The six are cut from the strip; Sesame and Biscuit are painted in code (`tools/paint-sesame.mjs`, `paint-biscuit.mjs`, both on `paint-kit.mjs`'s brush, light and grain), baked with `bake-pets --only <id>` and `--baby --only <id>`.

## Rooms (`src/modules/`, `src/world/`)
- `modules/`: `reading-comprehension`, `para-jumbles`, `para-summary`, `odd-one-out`, `word-dna`, `language-garden` (the Rootwood, six-beat sessions), `verbal-bank` (sentence placement, para completion, word bank, critical reasoning; route `#/bank/session/:type/:set`).
- `core/engine/`: one session engine per kind (`PracticeSession`, `BankSession`, `PJ/PS/OOO/WDSession`); all share the draft contract (`draft-shape.js`).
- `world/`: `regions.js` places, `lexicon.js` vocabulary rounds, `rewards.js`, `stage.js` painted room behind lessons, `audio.js` composed theme + ambience + friend voices (no song or ambience at all while a reading passage is open: `quietWorld`, set from `app.js` for `#/rc/session/` and the second look), `screens/` (place, round, Gauntlet/wilds, result, records, `library.js`).
- The Reading House is Chai's full-screen library (`place.js`, `world/screens/library.js`, `ui/styles/library.css`). `place.js` waits for `libraryStyle()` before rendering, then hands it `journey.js libraryShelves(content.rc, sessions)`: Foundation at the bottom, Elite at the top, a stable numbered book per passage, `clean` = best share right, shelves opening at `SHELF_OPENS_AT` of the shelf below read, and `owl` = the next book. Each shelf has its own leather palette and emblem. Read books lose most dust and gain ribbons; perfect reads have golden spines; fully read shelves gain keepsakes, all derived from existing records. Chai is a keyboard/touch control for progress and reading hints, reads after inactivity, sleeps later, and celebrates restored books. A floor ledger shows completed books, current reading level, five shelf indicators and the next milestone; Find my book brings the next passage into view and focuses it. Stars and the detailed rules live in the info popover. `catos:shelf` is only a last-look snapshot for arrival, wipe, unlock and ladder animations (three changes at most). Every book remains a DOM link with roving keyboard focus. `session.js` enforces the existing shelf lock for direct links too and results return to the library. Village entry still goes straight into the room; `nextFor('chai')` still returns its next book. The second look (`#/rc/second-look`) remains available by route.

## Learning core (`src/core/`)
- `content-loader/` registry, schema validation (`loader.js consistencyIssues`), library warm-up. `learning/`: `journey.js` (RC stages, `STAGE_SIZE`), `order.js` (per-learner order inside a level), `review.js` (skill/trap/pattern ledgers, due skills), `noticing.js` (the only code that turns ledgers into a sentence), `draft.js`, `taxonomy.js` (mirror of `content/taxonomy/varc-taxonomy.json`; verify §19 fails on drift). `mentor/` voice. `engagement/` XP, streaks, achievements. `storage/` adapter + IndexedDB. `router/`, `utils/`, `ads/rewarded.js` (the explanation lock, live in the Android app; §Shipping), `native.js` (the Android bridge).

## Content (`content/`)
JSON by type, one folder per type; `schema/` versioned; `taxonomy/` authority + `AUTHORING.md`; `index.json` registry; `manifest.json` + `boot-index.json` generated. Banks are not precached; `library-sync.js` warms them. Types: RC, PJ, PS, OOO, SP, PC, WB, CR, Word DNA, vocabulary, lexicon, twins, loanwords, Language Garden.

## UI (`src/ui/`, `src/shell/`)
Tokens and CSS in `ui/styles/` (`home.css` village, `rewards.css`, `world.css` rooms and sole owner of `--g-*` colours, `game.css` geometry/type/motion only). Web Components in `ui/components/`. `ui/modal.js` dialog contract. `ui/info.js` native-popover info buttons. `shell/` Progress, Settings (backup, Start over), preferences.

Small Android screens are the primary layout: settings controls stack below their labels on narrow phones; lesson navigation and selectors keep 44 px targets; the thinking coach lives beside the instruction instead of floating over answers. `ui/focus.js` carries focus to new questions, verdicts and completed lessons. `ui/modal.js` keeps focus and scrolling inside the active sheet, restores its trigger, and exports `dismissTopLayer` for `window.__catosBack`: the Android activity asks this before navigating history, so Back closes a popover or sheet first. Explicitly hidden content stays hidden regardless of component display rules. Vocabulary rounds wait for Next word after feedback; the Gauntlet retains its timed advance. Repeat links carry a fresh query, and pending lesson results cannot replace a screen the player has left. Practice clocks refresh at 250 ms (the second look at one second), independent of scoring timestamps.

## Shipping
Static files; GitHub Pages deploys the whole repo on push to `main` (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`). `service-worker.js` installs in resumable batches, promotes transactionally; cache ids are fingerprints of shipped files. `tools/build-precache.mjs` rewrites the lists; `build-manifest.mjs` the content manifest. `privacy.html` (root) is the policy Play links to.

**The Android app** (`android/`, package `com.nakulcreations.catos`, Google Play). One Java activity: a WebView serving the same files from inside the APK (`app/build.gradle` `copyWeb` syncs `index.html`, `src/`, `content/`, `assets/` into assets `www/`; `WebViewAssetLoader` serves them at `https://appassets.androidplatform.net/www/`), so there is no service worker and no library sync in the app (`app.js` returns before both when `inApp`). `Bridge.java` is `window.CatOSAndroid`, the page's only door to it being `src/core/native.js` (`ask(method, ...)` for calls answered later via `window.__catosNative`, `catos:native` events for `pro`/`net`). `Ads.java` = AdMob rewarded (the app's only ad), behind Google's UMP consent form in the EEA/UK/CH (`privacyOptionsRequired`/`showPrivacyOptions` feed Settings' "Privacy choices"), `Billing.java` = Play Billing for Pro (`catos_pro_monthly` and `catos_pro_yearly` subscriptions, `catos_pro_lifetime` one-time; Pro removes every ad; entitlement cached in preferences so Pro plays offline), `Cloud.java` = Sign in with Google (Credential Manager → Firebase Auth) and one cloud save per account (Firestore `saves/{uid}`, the backup JSON gzipped; rules: only that uid). Firebase project `cat-os-612e8` (`app/google-services.json`, git-ignored) also gives Google Analytics: the page sends `screen_view` per route, `login`, `share` (`track()` in `native.js`); installs, purchases and ad events are automatic. Web side of the cloud save: `shell/account.js` (save on leaving the app when something changed; a phone with its own village chooses before anything is overwritten; `catos:cloud-linked` holds the uid this phone may save to, so Start over never pushes an empty village). Web side: `core/ads/rewarded.js` (`isUnlocked`, `unlockWithAd`, `gated(html)` for HTML-string screens, `lockCard`; a video opens a 20-minute pass and fires `catos:why-open`, which opens every lock on screen), `<cat-explanation>` locks itself, `shell/online-gate.js` (free player offline = wall), `shell/pro.js` (`#/pro`), `shell/intro.js` (the skippable Nakul Creations opening video, `assets/intro/`, app only: `index.html` raises `html[data-intro]` before first paint when the bridge is there, `catos:intro` = `on`/`off` overrides; the village song and first-visit hello wait for it via `hushWorld` and `whenIntroDone`; `MainActivity` lets the WebView play its sound without a tap). On the web none of it locks anything. Gate: `tools/check-app-shell.mjs` (a fake bridge). Store images: `tools/store/render.mjs` → `android/store/`.

## Gates (`tools/verify.mjs`, plain Node, no dependencies)
Sections check content/schema/registry, precache hash (§25), module graph, engines, economy (§16 `check-pet-economy`, pet lines `check-pets`), corpus QC (§21), taxonomy mirror (§19). Browser sections use `tools/cdp-lite.mjs` (any Chrome/Edge, prints SKIPPED if none): §22 `check-contrast`, §23 `check-village-data`, §23b `check-village`, §24 `check-rendered-contrast`, §26 `check-hostile-records`, §27 `check-noticing`, §28 `check-reach` (Tab/focus/44px), §29 `check-interruption`, §30 `check-resume`. Content tools: `check-content`, `build-index`, `qc-corpus`, `blind-solve`, `option-tells`, `shuffle-answers`, `build-lexicon` (reads `../KNOWLEDGE/99_REFERENCE`, which must stay).

`tools/check-player-experience.mjs` (§31) adds hit-tested end-to-end reading, vocabulary and Gauntlet completion/replay; verbal-only progress; coach and Android Back dismissal; reflection editing; backup/reset cancellation; and clock redraw limits. Its full matrix covers the reachable rooms, guides and learning pages in both themes, with extra checks at 320 px, 360 px, keyboard-height, landscape and desktop sizes. Set `CATOS_UX_SHOTS` to save JPEG previews; `--flows-only` skips the matrix. Browser commands release their timeout handles and fail promptly on disconnection. The rendered-contrast gate fails if any requested screen could not be measured.
