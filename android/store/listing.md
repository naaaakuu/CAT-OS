**Full description:**

CAT OS turns CAT VARC preparation into a small painted village that grows every time you get better.

Eight friends live in the village, one for each part of the verbal section. Answer real CAT-style questions and the friend who teaches that subject grows up, their house grows, and the village comes alive. Nothing grows for time spent. It grows for learning.

WHAT YOU PRACTISE
• Reading Comprehension: original passages in the CAT register, from first steps to 99th-percentile difficulty, timed like the exam.
• Para Jumbles: put the sentences in the author's order, then see why each join holds.
• Para Summary: find the summary that keeps the author's point, not just the words.
• Odd One Out: spot the sentence that breaks the paragraph.
• Sentence Placement, Para Completion, Word Bank and Critical Reasoning sets.
• Vocabulary rounds and word families that stick.

EXPLANATIONS THAT TEACH
After every answer you see whether you were right and what the answer was. Open the full explanation to learn why your pick tempted you, why the answer holds, and how every other option was built to trap you, plus one reading habit to carry into the next question.

A MENTOR THAT NOTICES
CAT OS remembers the traps you fall for and the skills you are building, and brings back the right questions at the right time.

FREE, WITH ONE KIND OF AD
CAT OS is free. The only ad is a short video you choose to watch to open full explanations, and one video opens every explanation for 20 minutes. No banners. No pop-ups. The free app needs an internet connection.

CAT OS PRO
No videos, every explanation open, and the village plays offline. One year or lifetime.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account and no sign-up. Your progress lives on your phone. Export a backup any time.

CAT OS is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with the IIMs or the Common Admission Test.
